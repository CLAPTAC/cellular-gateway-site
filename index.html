Built for operators who want control

Start with a reliable device link, then grow into messaging, calling, and multi-SIM routing.

  • Secure device channel

    Android gateways register with dedicated credentials and stay connected over authenticated WebSockets with heartbeats and auto-reconnect.

  • SMS and voice commands

    Send SMS, receive inbound messages, place calls, and track status through versioned APIs and a management portal.

  • Multi-device routing

    Group phones, auto-select healthy devices, queue when offline, and fail over across SIMs and carriers.

  • Self-hosted by design

    Run on your own Linux host with Docker or local Python/Node. Keep numbers, traffic, and credentials inside your boundary.

Open stack, practical pieces

FastAPI gateway, Next.js portal, Kotlin Android app, PostgreSQL or SQLite for local development.

  • QR provisioning

    Create a device in the portal, scan once on the phone, skip typing long tokens.

  • Operator dashboard

    Watch online status, battery, messages, calls, API keys, and routing groups.

  • Documented protocol

    REST + WebSocket contracts so you can integrate any language or automation.
