SMS

Send outbound SMS through a phone's SIM, receive inbound SMS, and track message status and failures.

What works#

  • POST /api/v1/messages creates an outbound SMS command and a message record.
  • An online phone sends the text with Android's SmsManager, splitting long messages into parts.
  • The message moves to SENT or FAILED, and SMS_SENT or SMS_FAILED webhooks fire.
  • Inbound SMS on the phone are reported to the server and stored as INBOUND messages, and an SMS_RECEIVED webhook fires.
  • The dashboard's Messages page shows history and can send a test SMS.

Phone setup#

  1. Install and connect the Android app and scan the device QR code.
  2. Allow SMS permissions when prompted (send, receive and read SMS).
  3. Keep the app running. Disable battery optimization for it, and on some manufacturers (Xiaomi and similar) also allow autostart and background activity.

Send a message#

curl
curl -X POST https://gateway.example.com/api/v1/messages \
  -H "X-API-Key: $API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "device": "dev_YOUR_DEVICE_KEY",
    "to": "+15551234567",
    "message": "Hello from Cellular Gateway"
  }'
Python
import os, requests

r = requests.post(
    "https://gateway.example.com/api/v1/messages",
    headers={"X-API-Key": os.environ["GATEWAY_API_KEY"]},
    json={"device": "dev_YOUR_DEVICE_KEY", "to": "+15551234567", "message": "Hello"},
    timeout=15,
)
r.raise_for_status()

Use group instead of device to let the gateway choose a phone, with queueing and failover. See Multi-device routing and the field reference.

Track the result#

bash
curl https://gateway.example.com/api/v1/messages/$MESSAGE_ID -H "X-API-Key: $API_KEY"
StatusMeaning
QUEUEDWaiting for a phone to come online
SENDINGHanded to a phone
SENTThe phone's radio accepted the message
DELIVEREDDelivery confirmed (best effort, not normalized across carriers)
FAILEDThe phone reported an error. Check error_message
RECEIVEDAn inbound message

Receive messages#

Inbound SMS arrive as SMS_RECEIVED webhooks (body truncated to 200 characters) and are stored in full. Read them with GET /api/v1/messages and look for direction: "INBOUND".

Notes#

  • Without queue or a group, an offline target device returns 409.
  • Carrier SMS charges and Android's sending limits apply. Android may throttle or prompt on bursts of outgoing SMS.
  • Delivery receipts from carriers are not fully supported. Treat SENT as accepted for sending, not delivered.
  • There is no idempotency key. If your request times out and you retry, the SMS may be sent twice, so deduplicate on your side.
